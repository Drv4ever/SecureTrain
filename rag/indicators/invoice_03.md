---
id: invoice_03
tactic: invoice
title: Unexpected Order Confirmation and Receipt
source: SOUPS 2020; CISA Phishing Indicators
technique: Reverse Phishing / Fraud Inquiry
---
The victim receives a billing notification or invoice for high-value hardware or software that they did not order, accompanied by a prompt to 'dispute charge' or 'cancel order' by clicking a link or downloading an attached macro-enabled document. The target's natural impulse is to correct the financial error immediately to protect corporate funds. The dispute portal is a credential harvester designed to capture SSO credentials. Charges should be cross-referenced through internal accounting rather than external links.
