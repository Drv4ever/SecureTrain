id: invoice_02
tactic: invoice
title: Overdue Service Interruption Notices
source: Reinheimer et al., SOUPS 2020
technique: Supply Chain Disruption Threat
---
This tactic claims an overdue balance on critical business infrastructure (such as cloud hosting, enterprise software licenses, or logistics carriers) and threatens service suspension within 24 to 48 hours. Operational personnel fear responsibility for production outages and are prompted to quickly click the 'settle invoice' link. Threat actors rely on organizational silos between the requester and accounts payable. Invoices should always be reconciled against existing purchase orders in the internal ERP system.
