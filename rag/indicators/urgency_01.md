---
id: urgency_01
tactic: urgency
title: Artificial Expiration Windows
source: Reinheimer et al., SOUPS 2020; NIST SP 800-177
technique: Fear of Access Loss
---
Attackers deliberately introduce tight, artificial countdowns (such as 10 to 30 minutes) to induce cognitive overload and bypass deliberate decision-making. These notices claim that essential corporate services, VPN tunnels, or directory credentials will expire unless immediate validation occurs. Legitimate IT departments almost universally provide several days of advance notice and offer out-of-band portal access rather than requiring urgent link clicks. High perceived urgency significantly suppresses critical evaluation of the sender domain and link targets.
