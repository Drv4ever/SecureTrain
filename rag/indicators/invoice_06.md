tactic: invoice
title: Fiscal Year-End Tax and Audit Invoicing
source: FBI IC3 Alerts; SOUPS 2020
technique: Seasonal Accounting Pressure
---
During quarterly or fiscal year-end closing, accounting departments experience heightened workloads and tight reconciliation deadlines. Attackers capitalize on this fatigue by submitting urgent 'final reconciliations' or tax compliance invoices. The pressure to close books rapidly reduces adherence to multi-party approval controls. Finance teams must maintain strict dual-authorization controls regardless of seasonal accounting deadlines.
