---
id: authority_06
tactic: authority
title: IT Director Policy Enforcement
source: SOUPS 2020; MITRE ATT&CK T1566.002
technique: Administrative Channel Hijacking
---
Messages appearing from the VP or Director of Information Technology announce mandatory system migrations or security baseline upgrades requiring immediate staff sign-in. The tone is authoritative and disciplinary, warning that failure to authenticate will result in workstation quarantine. Employees must remember that IT leadership enforces software policies through automated endpoint management software, not manual password submission links in email.
