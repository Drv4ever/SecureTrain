id: urgency_05_auth
tactic: authority
title: Internal Audit and Risk Committee Requests
source: NIST SP 800-53 AC-6; SOUPS 2020
technique: Internal Oversight Exploitation
---
This indicator leverages internal audit and ethics committee impersonation, demanding that the employee complete a confidential self-assessment or upload role permissions to an external compliance tracker. Employees view audit groups as authoritative and compulsory, reducing suspicion. By exploiting compliance fear, adversaries capture directory credentials. Genuine internal audit procedures operate within documented internal auditing software and enterprise ticket queues.
