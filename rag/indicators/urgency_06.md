---
id: urgency_06
tactic: urgency
title: Executive Demands for Immediate Action
source: CISA Phishing Awareness Guidance
technique: Time-Sensitive Management Requests
---
This attack pattern combines urgency with perceived hierarchy, claiming an executive is currently in an urgent meeting or traveling and requires immediate file review or task completion. The lure stresses that delays will disrupt high-stakes negotiations or executive board presentations. Time compression impairs the victim's willingness to question anomalies in sender address or tone. Organizations should enforce strict dual-channel verification protocols for high-priority requests.
