---
id: credential_06
tactic: credential
title: HR Portal Password Synchronization
source: SOUPS 2020 Phishing Taxonomy
technique: Unified Directory Credential Theft
---
This pattern informs the employee that their HR self-service portal, payroll profile, and corporate email passwords have become desynchronized. The message provides a 'synchronization portal' where the employee is instructed to provide their current corporate password to unify directory access. Legitimate identity management systems synchronize hashes across back-end directory services without ever requesting end-user password entry in external forms.
