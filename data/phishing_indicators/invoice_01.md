tactic: invoice
title: Fraudulent Vendor Bank Account Redirection
source: MITRE ATT&CK T1566.001; FBI IC3 BEC Report
technique: Bank Detail Modification (BEC)
---
Adversaries target finance, procurement, and operations staff by claiming that an existing, trusted vendor has changed banking or wire remittance instructions due to an acquisition or audit. The message attaches an authentic-looking invoice PDF and instructs accounts payable to direct future payments to a new bank account. Attackers frequently register lookalike vendor domains with single-letter variations. Financial policies must mandate out-of-band phone verification using known-good phone numbers before altering disbursement accounts.
