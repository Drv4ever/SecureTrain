id: credential_03
tactic: credential
title: Quarantined Message and Secure Mailbox Traps
source: SOUPS 2020; NIST SP 800-177
technique: Secure Gateway Impersonation
---
The victim receives a notification that several encrypted or incoming messages have been held in the corporate email security quarantine. To release or preview the messages, the recipient is instructed to log into the 'secure viewer portal' using their corporate email credentials. Because employees fear missing vital client communications, they readily attempt authentication. Actual email gateways deliver digest reports that do not require credential submission on third-party domains.
