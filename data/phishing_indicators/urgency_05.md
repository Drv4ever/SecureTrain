---
id: urgency_05
tactic: urgency
title: Impending Account Deactivation
source: SOUPS 2020 Phishing Taxonomy
technique: Service Termination Pressure
---
This pattern warns the employee that their inbox, cloud drive, or workspace license has exceeded storage limits or retention policies and is scheduled for immediate deletion. The message emphasizes irreversible data loss if action is not taken within hours. By provoking panic over loss of historical work and documents, the adversary redirects the victim to a credential harvesting form. Standard enterprise provisioning platforms queue notifications weeks in advance rather than threatening abrupt termination.
