---
id: urgency_04
tactic: urgency
title: Immediate CI/CD and Pipeline Blockage
source: MITRE ATT&CK T1566; OWASP Top 10 CI/CD
technique: Developer Workflow Interruption
---
Targeting technical staff, this indicator presents a simulated panic where developer tokens or deployment credentials have allegedly failed, freezing build pipelines or staging environments. Because engineering personnel prioritize uptime and deployment continuity, they are susceptible to entering access keys into fraudulent mirrors of GitHub, GitLab, or cloud console portals. Critical infrastructure credentials should never be re-authenticated through unsolicited email alerts.
