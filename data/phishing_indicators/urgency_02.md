id: urgency_02
tactic: urgency
title: Critical Security Incident Alarms
source: MITRE ATT&CK T1566.002; CISA Security Advisory
technique: False Security Alert
---
Adversaries fabricate alerts alleging immediate account compromise, malware detection, or anomalous logins from foreign IP addresses. The recipient is urged to click immediately to 'secure the account' or 'quarantine the threat' before wider damage occurs. In reality, actual SOC automated workflows isolate assets at the endpoint detection level without demanding end-user password re-entry on external links. Employees should independently contact the internal security operations center rather than acting on embedded links.
