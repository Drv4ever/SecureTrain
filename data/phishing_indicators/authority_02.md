id: authority_02
tactic: authority
title: External Legal and Regulatory Mandates
source: MITRE ATT&CK T1566.001
technique: Legal Hold / Regulatory Intimidation
---
This indicator utilizes legal intimidation by masquerading as outside legal counsel, regulatory bodies, or compliance auditors issuing a formal legal hold notice. The message commands the recipient to preserve documents or log into an external deposition repository under threat of statutory penalties. The formal legal phrasing and perceived legal liability discourage scrutiny. Valid litigation notices are routed through official internal corporate legal departments rather than unsolicited external links.
