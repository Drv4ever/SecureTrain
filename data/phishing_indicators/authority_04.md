tactic: authority
title: Board of Directors Inquiries
source: CISA Business Email Compromise Guide
technique: Governance Level Impersonation
---
The adversary presents as a member of the Board of Directors or Chairman requesting immediate off-the-record information regarding company performance, strategic assets, or vendor relationships. The request relies on the employee's reluctance to question senior governance figures. Attackers often utilize lookalike domains with slight character substitutions. Employees should verify board inquiries through authorized corporate secretary or communications channels.
