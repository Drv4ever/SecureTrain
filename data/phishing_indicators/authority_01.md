---
id: authority_01
tactic: authority
title: C-Suite Restructuring Impersonation
source: Reinheimer et al., SOUPS 2020
technique: Executive Impersonation
---
Adversaries spoof executive leadership (such as the CEO or Chief Operating Officer) requesting confidential assistance with sensitive corporate restructuring or non-disclosure initiatives. The communication explicitly demands secrecy, instructing the recipient not to discuss the email with managers or teammates to avoid premature disclosure. Exploiting professional deference to authority, the attacker maneuvers the target into opening malicious attachments or sharing internal files. Corporate policies must clarify that legitimate executives will never ask staff to violate standard governance.
